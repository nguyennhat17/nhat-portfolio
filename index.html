BEHIND THE SCENES / THE GREATEST SYMPHONY

From a musical idea to our first game.

Introduction

The Greatest Symphony was our team’s first experience creating a video game in Unity, writing C# scripts in Visual Studio. We learned how to bring a story, levels and gameplay together through a shared development process.

Our story grew from music as a means of self-expression, and the tension between pursuing a dream and meeting family expectations. Forte discovers his late father’s guitar and unfinished compositions, inspiring him to pursue music despite his mother’s wish for a different future. That conflict shaped a journey about grief, determination and finding a personal voice.

BEHIND THE SCENES / ROAD FROM FED-UP TO HERO

A story told through play.

Introduction

Road from Fed-up to Hero follows a gamer struggling with frustration around his studies and school life. Our two-person team set out to tell his story through a narrative-driven 2D pixel-art game, weaving playable mini-games into his journey.

We wanted the player to take part in his study sessions as the story progressed. Chemistry gave us a starting point for puzzles that could feel engaging while fitting the character’s everyday challenges.

DESIGN INTENT

Make each study session a playable part of the story, with short chemistry challenges that give the player something to recognise, compare or build.

Chemistry as gameplay

We explored three ways to turn chemistry concepts into mini-games. Each uses a different kind of interaction, bringing variety to the study sessions while keeping them connected to the same subject.

01 / Lightest to heaviest

The first mini-game players encounter asks them to arrange elements in order of atomic mass. The balance imagery frames the task around comparing and ordering, giving this mini-game a different rhythm from the timed catching challenge.

Design aim Introduce a more deliberate puzzle based on relationships between elements.

02 / Build the molecule

Bring atoms together to assemble the requested compound, such as carbon dioxide. The player works with the parts of a formula rather than only reading its name.

Design aim Turn a chemistry formula into a construction puzzle the player can interact with.

03 / Element hunt

Inspired by Aim Lab on Steam, Element Hunt connects the protagonist’s obsession with FPS games to his chemistry studies. Players catch the required chemical elements before time runs out, turning element recognition into a targeting challenge that draws on his interest in aiming and quick reactions.

Design aim Bring the character’s FPS interests into his study sessions through a short, aim-focused chemistry challenge.

BEHIND THE SCENES / HERO ACADEMY

The process of making the game.

Introduction

Hero Academy began as a team assignment to create a game with Unity’s physics at the heart of its design. We started with a simple bouncing-ball idea, which evolved into a disc that the player could throw and interact with.

After testing the mechanic, we developed a map layout and built the concept into a roguelike game. The bouncing disc became the core combat mechanic, allowing players to defeat enemies with a thrown disc that could rebound and hit other enemies.

BEHIND THE SCENES / DREAM CATCHER

From cultural symbol to playful protection.

Introduction

This assignment asked our team to research a cultural symbol and translate its meaning into a game. We chose the dreamcatcher. My starting point was to understand the Ojibwe story associated with it, then explore how its protective purpose could shape the player’s actions.

In the account explored in our research, Spider Woman’s protection is carried forward by mothers and grandmothers weaving willow-hoop dreamcatchers for children. Good dreams pass through, while bad dreams are caught and disappear with the morning sun. This became the starting point for our game’s focus on keeping nightmares away from a sleeping child.

01 / Turning meaning into mechanics

My idea was to create a short lane-defense game inspired by Plants vs. Zombies. The dreamcatcher’s protective role becomes a game system: collect positive energy represented by toys, buy dreamcatcher cards, and place them on a grid to intercept nightmares.

CORE LOOP / MY DESIGN IDEA

Collect energy → Choose a card → Place a dreamcatcher → Block or clear nightmares → Protect the child.

02 / Building a nighttime world

I proposed a calm, cozy bedroom setting, with a grandmother introducing the dreamcatcher’s purpose. Early room concepts drew visual inspiration from Night of the Full Moon, with Unity and Aseprite used in the project workflow.

03 / Design direction

The team’s goal was to make protection the central theme. A compact resource-and-placement loop connects the symbol to what the player does, while the room, characters and dream imagery give those actions a narrative setting.

THE STUDY / APPROACH & MATERIALS

Understanding the player experience.

01 / Research question

How does The Sims connect everyday needs, social relationships and player choices to immersion? Our study considered Maslow’s hierarchy of needs as one lens for examining the franchise.

02 / Comparative approach

We compared the series through game design, learning curves, social dynamics, world-building and audiovisual presentation, bringing observations together in an academic report.
